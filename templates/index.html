About

I trained in political science and international studies, then in computational social sciences.

Since then I have worked on EU trade statistics at Eurostat, on science diplomacy at the Embassy of Spain in Belgium, and on policy evaluation for the European Commission’s DG COMM. In each role the job was similar: take public data that is hard to read, analyse it carefully, and present it so non-specialists can use it.
